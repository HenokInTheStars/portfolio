SMS Spam Detection Engine

High-precision text classification pipeline trained to detect SMS spam with near-zero false alarms on legitimate messages.

The Problem

SMS spam filters that prioritize catching every junk text often misclassify critical one-time passwords and personal messages as spam.

What I Built

An NLP classification pipeline combining tokenization, n-gram TF-IDF extraction, and Multinomial Naive Bayes, calibrated for high precision.

My Stack: Python Scikit-Learn TF-IDF (3,000 features) MultinomialNB (α=0.5) Pandas Jupyter Notebook

One Hard Technical Decision & Why: Tuned the decision threshold and Laplace smoothing parameter (α=0.5) to enforce 99.24% precision at the cost of 87.25% recall. In user-facing SMS filtering, missing ~13% of spam is an acceptable tradeoff to protect legitimate personal and transactional messages from false positives.

Results (Stated Honestly): Evaluated on an 80/20 stratified split of 5,572 labeled messages (about 13% spam): Accuracy 98.21%, Precision 99.24%, Recall 87.25%, F1-Score 92.86%.

What I'd Improve Next: Evaluate character-level n-grams and DistilBERT embeddings to improve recall on heavily obfuscated or misspelled spam words.

2-YEAR DATASET

Time-Series Anomaly Detection Suite

Statistical anomaly detection framework analyzing two years of daily sales data to diagnose outlier masking and level shifts.

The Problem

Standard Z-score thresholding fails when extreme spikes inflate standard deviation, creating false negatives (masking) on genuine operational anomalies.

What I Built

A statistical evaluation suite implementing Modified Z-Score with Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) and KNN spatial distance outlier scoring.

My Stack: Python Pandas NumPy Scikit-Learn Matplotlib & Seaborn

One Hard Technical Decision & Why: Adopted Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) with a 50% breakdown point instead of mean/standard deviation. This guarantees robust detection even when up to 50% of the window is contaminated by sales spikes or zero-inventory stockout days.

Results (Stated Honestly): Evaluated on 2 years of daily retail data across 4 product categories, successfully classifying point spikes, stockouts, level shifts, and gradual drift.

What I'd Improve Next: Implement streaming change-point detection (such as CUSUM) for automated operational alerts.
